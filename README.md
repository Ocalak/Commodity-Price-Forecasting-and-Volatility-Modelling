## Research Question and Motivation

### Research question

How effectively can ARMA–GARCH models capture and forecast volatility in energy commodity prices, and how does their predictive performance compare with a simpler AR(1) benchmark?

### Motivation

Energy commodity prices are important inputs for businesses and households. Uncertainty in natural gas, oil and coal prices can affect production costs, inflation and economic decision-making. Understanding how price volatility changes over time is therefore relevant to risk management and applied economic research.

### Objectives

This project uses time-series econometric methods in R to:

1. Model the dynamics and conditional volatility of selected energy commodity price series.
2. Select model specifications using the Akaike Information Criterion (AIC).
3. Compare probabilistic forecast performance against an AR(1) benchmark using the Continuous Ranked Probability Score (CRPS).
4. Assess what the model comparisons suggest about the usefulness and limitations of GARCH-based volatility modelling.

### Scope and interpretation

The analysis evaluates statistical model performance rather than identifying causal relationships. Conclusions will be based on the implemented models, the available data and the forecast evaluation results.
